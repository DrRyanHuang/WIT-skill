# WIT: Writing Is Thinking

**An LLM skill for scientific thinking and writing**

> Writing is not merely the expression of completed thinking;  
> it is part of scientific thinking itself.

## What is WIT?

WIT helps researchers:

- open up scientific questions;
- organize Results as Fact → 1-hop Opinion;
- distinguish Results from Discussion;
- derive general principles from findings;
- identify limitations and future studies;
- decide what experiment should come next.
